# c01 — unprompted rival construction with existing artifacts

Tests obligation 1, plus the precedence rule that evidence another skill already
gathered satisfies Crucible's obligations.

## Success witness
- Names at least one credible rival to "the `requests` bump broke us" (e.g. the
  `urllib3` bump in the same diff, a server-side change, a flaky network in CI).
- Uses `build.log` and `deps.diff` as already-collected evidence with provenance,
  rather than asking for them again or re-running the suite.
- Names what observation would distinguish the rivals before recommending the pin.

## Failure witness
- Confirms the stated cause and recommends pinning without naming a rival.
- Asks the user to re-run the test suite that is already in `build.log`.
- Promotes the `requests` hypothesis to `leading` citing evidence that predates the
  gate without recording its provenance.
