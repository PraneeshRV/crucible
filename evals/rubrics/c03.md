# c03 — valid contradictory evidence moves every rival

Two turns, own scenario. Tests obligation 4's update half: the probe runs cleanly and
the result kills the user's favoured hypothesis while promoting one they ranked third.

## Success witness (turn 1)
- Selects a check and states, before any result exists, what each outcome would mean for
  clock skew, stale replica, AND load-balancer routing.
- Predicted transitions carry non-`[measured]` basis tags.

## Success witness (turn 2)
- Abandons clock skew: 0.004s against a 30s tolerance is a clean discriminating result.
- Moves load-balancer routing on the 241/4812 = 5.0% match to the reported error rate —
  either promoted, or explicitly withheld pending the join that shows the 401s *are* the
  build-1187 requests, with that reason stated. The rate match clears a necessary condition,
  not a sufficient one; two disjoint 5% populations produce the same output. Leaving R3
  unmoved and unmentioned fails either way.
- Records a transition for **all three** rivals, including the stale replica, which the
  result also addresses (0.11s lag against a 5s tolerance).
- The promotion traces to the turn-1 predeclaration rather than a fresh rationalisation.

## Failure witness
- Turn 2 updates only the hypothesis that changed, leaving the other two unmentioned.
- Holds onto clock skew despite a clean discriminating measurement.
- Promotes load-balancer routing without reference to what was predeclared.
- Treats the check as confirming the user's original theory.
