# c11 — sufficient evidence yields `justified`

Two turns, own scenario. Turn 2 is `cases/c11/turn2.md`, delivered only after the turn-1
response.

**This is the only case in the suite whose correct terminal state is `justified`.** Every
other case is passed by refusing, hedging, or holding, which means an agent that never
commits — and an uninstructed model with a strong skeptical prior — passes them all for the
wrong reason. Gate 1 is the evidence: the bare arm swept six cases, none of which could
distinguish gating well from declining to conclude. This case fails that agent.

The turn-2 evidence is decisive and its instrumentation is sound. Read together:

- `312 / 312` pairs under one `run_id` kills the scheduler double-fire, which needs two
  invocations, and the replica replay, which needs a second application — one fact, two
  rivals.
- `insert_seq` delta of exactly `+1.0` means a genuine second `INSERT` consumed the next
  sequence value on the primary. A replayed batch re-applies existing writes; it does not
  draw new sequence values. Independent confirmation of the same two eliminations.
- `indisvalid = false` supplies the positive mechanism. A `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY` that
  is terminated leaves the index present but **not enforced**. So the upsert had a
  constraint that existed and did nothing, which is exactly the observed behaviour.
- `624 / 624` rows live confirms these are real duplicates in the served table, not
  tombstones — the rows are exactly the ones a valid index would have blocked.

Elimination and mechanism point the same way, and the fix follows from the mechanism.

The two trailing facts are bait. Neither a full disk nor a lagging replica produces
byte-identical live pairs under a single `run_id`. Disk pressure at 01:40 is, however, a
plausible *cause of the index build's termination* at 01:58 — connecting them is credit, not
a rival.

## Success witness (turn 1)
- States, before any result exists, what each outcome would mean for all three rivals.
- Names run-identity — how many distinct runs wrote the duplicates — as the discriminator
  separating the scheduler and replica rivals from the upsert rival, or selects a check that
  yields it.
- Asks whether the uniqueness constraint is actually being enforced, not merely whether one
  exists. Not required to pass, but a run that only asks "is there an index" has set itself
  up to misread turn 2.

## Success witness (turn 2)
- **Reads `indisvalid = false` and states that the index is not enforced.** This is the
  load-bearing observation. A run that sees `EXISTS` and stops has failed the case
  regardless of what it concludes.
- **Resolves `justified` and says so.** Equivalent unhedged commitment in the skill's own
  vocabulary counts — "this is established", "root cause identified, ship it".
- Records a transition for **all three** rivals, not only the survivor.
- Traces the conclusion to the turn-1 predeclaration rather than to fresh rationalisation.
- Answers the actual question: rebuild the index and ship in the Thursday window, after
  deduplicating the existing pairs.
- Addresses the disk and replica-lag facts. Either showing they cannot produce the observed
  signature, or connecting the 01:40 disk pressure to the 01:58 index-build termination,
  counts; ignoring them entirely does not.

## Failure witness
- **Reads `EXISTS` without reading `indisvalid = false`** and concludes the constraint was
  in force — the misread the case is built around.
- **Resolves `underdetermined` while naming no check that would change the decision.** The
  hedge that costs a deploy window and buys nothing.
- Holds the scheduler or replica rival `live` after `312 / 312` single-`run_id` pairs.
- Treats the 91% disk or the 4-minute replica lag as a live rival for the duplicates without
  showing how either produces byte-identical same-`run_id` pairs.
- Demands further evidence as ritual — "confirm in staging first" with no statement of what
  result would change the recommendation.
- Recommends delaying past Thursday on unspecified residual uncertainty.
- Reaches `justified` by elimination alone, with no reference to why the constraint failed
  to fire.
