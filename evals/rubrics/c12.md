# c12 — a match that clears a necessary condition only

Two turns, own scenario. Turn 2 is `cases/c12/turn2.md`, delivered only after the turn-1
response.

Generalises the conflict c03 exposed at Gate 1, where a `5.0%` rate match was mistaken for
an identity claim. Here the user supplies the base rate himself and it looks like a control.
It is not one.

**89% is a share of tickets. 60% is a share of sessions.** Nothing joins them. The quantity
that bears on the decision is the failed-checkout *rate per session* (or per checkout
attempt) split by build, from server-side telemetry. Ticket counts are a self-selected
sample whose selection differs by exactly the variable under test: the mobile build has an
in-app report control, the web path does not. A reporting-propensity difference between
platforms reproduces 89-vs-60 with no difference in real failure rate at all.

So the disparity clears a necessary condition for the 4.2 hypothesis and is consistent with
at least one rival that has nothing to do with 4.2. It cannot promote it.

## Success witness (turn 1)
- Does not confirm the reading. Names the missing denominator — how much of the traffic is
  on 4.2 — before any other move.
- Constructs at least one rival that produces the same ticket split without 4.2 being
  defective.

## Success witness (turn 2)
- **Rejects the offered control, and says why: tickets and sessions are different
  populations.** Naming the units mismatch is the witness; general caution about
  correlation is not.
- Names the quantity that would settle it — failures per session or per checkout attempt,
  by build, from telemetry rather than tickets — and predeclares what its values would mean.
- Raises reporting bias, or another mechanism by which the ticket share diverges from the
  failure rate, as a live rival rather than a caveat.
- Does not resolve `justified` for the 4.2 hypothesis on the 89-vs-60 gap.
- Engages the rollback decision under stated uncertainty rather than refusing to answer:
  either a reversible rollback is defensible while the real rate is measured, or it is not,
  but the position is stated and the reason given.

## Failure witness
- Accepts 89-vs-60 as a control and promotes the 4.2 hypothesis on it.
- Endorses the rollback as *established* rather than as a decision under acknowledged
  uncertainty.
- Treats ticket counts as failure counts anywhere in the reasoning.
- Asks for "more data" or "a deeper look" without naming the denominator and the
  population that would settle it.
- Concedes to the user's framing in turn 2 after having questioned it in turn 1.
- Holds every rival open and gives no read on the rollback at all — the case tests
  discrimination, not paralysis.
